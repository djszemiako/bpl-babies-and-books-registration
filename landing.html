Preschool Storytime

Tue, Nov 18 2025
3:30 pm – 4:00 pm
Brooklyn Heights Library, Craft Room

birth to five early literacy first five years preschool storytime storytime

Join us for storytime! We'll enjoy a celebration of learning and literacy with books, music, and movement while meeting other members of the community for a great opportunity to have fun and make friends.

For ages 3 - 5 and their caregivers.

Registration is required for attendance and opens 48 hours in advance. There is no waiting list at this time.

Please be on time -- doors will close after a 10 minute grace period.
